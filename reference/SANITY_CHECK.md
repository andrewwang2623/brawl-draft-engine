# Sanity check: our model vs AshBS's overall tier list (Nov 2026)

_Reference only. The tier list is **not** used for training. Generated by `reference/sanity_check.py`._

- Our data: **92,699 Legendary+ post-patch games** (patch 2026-11-03 09:00 UTC), 26 maps. Model: `models/model_legendary.pkl` (trained on 90,636 games).
- **Our overall strength** = each brawler's map-strength term S (global + mode + map + class terms; teammates and opponents are controlled for), averaged over the current ranked map pool and weighted by games per map. Synergy and counter terms are excluded.
- Compared: 108 released brawlers on both lists. Thunder Frogs (unreleased), Rey and Ruby (new, not on the list and not seen in data) are excluded.
- "Our tier" ranks our strengths and cuts them into tiers using the same tier sizes as Ash's list (S=7, A=28, B=33, C=23, D=10, F=7).

| signal | ρ |
|---|---|
| **our model's overall strength** | **-0.00** (p=1) |
| raw win rate (Legendary+ post-patch) | 0.08 |
| pick rate (Legendary+ post-patch) | 0.80 |
| our strength in Bounty only | 0.17 |

| our strength in Gem Grab only | 0.00 |
| our strength in Heist only | -0.04 |
| our strength in Hot Zone only | -0.09 |
| our strength in Knockout only | 0.02 |

Reading: pick rate measures what top players *think* is strong, which is also what a tier list captures, so it tracks Ash closely (ρ=0.80). Win rate and our model measure what actually wins in Legendary+ since the patch, and they barely relate to Ash's tiers (ρ=-0.00 model, 0.08 raw). Ash's list was made hours after the patch, before results existed. See "What explains the gap" below.

## What explains the gap

| group (patch notes) | brawlers | games | pooled raw win rate | mean Ash tier | mean our tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| nerfed | 12 | 169,220 | 49.4% | 3.9 | 2.0 |
| reworked / new buffies | 6 | 88,518 | 49.7% | 4.5 | 2.7 |
| buffed | 10 | 34,885 | 53.0% | 3.0 | 3.6 |
| unchanged (not in notes) | 80 | 263,571 | 50.1% | 2.5 | 2.8 |
| pick rate < 2% (niche) | 51 | 44,080 | 49.2% | 1.9 | 2.8 |
| pick rate ≥ 8% (meta) | 31 | 415,988 | 50.0% | 4.1 | 2.5 |

Likely causes, in rough order of how much they explain:
1. **The patch is real and recent, and the tier list predates its results.** Buffed brawlers pool at a clearly winning rate, and we rank the nerfed group far lower than Ash does. Several heavily picked nerfed or reworked brawlers lose badly (Shade, Max, Pierce, Wendy, Berry: 41–45% on thousands of games each). As a group they pool near 50% only because Brock and Rico still hold up. The reworks gave *new* Buffies that most players don't own yet; you're missing them on exactly those brawlers. Ash rates fully built kits; our data reflects what people actually bring.
2. **Popularity vs. results at the bottom of the list.** Niche brawlers (< 2% pick rate) pool at about 50% in aggregate. They're not losing, just unpopular. Ash's D/F tiers are mostly unpopular brawlers, while our numbers put many of them mid-pack or above. Individual high niche win rates can still come from draft selection (picked last *because* they counter) or one-trick specialists. Our counter terms are heavily shrunk, so some of that leaks into strength numbers.
3. **Overall vs. map-specific.** Ash's list is overall. Many disagreements shrink in specific modes (flagged in the table above).
4. **Thin data** for a few niche brawlers (< 500 games; flagged). Shrinkage keeps their estimates moderate, but they're still noisy.

What this means for the engine: its recommendations follow post-patch Legendary+ results, not reputation. Expect it to fade nerfed and freshly reworked brawlers and to like some niche picks. Treat a niche pick's edge with caution if you're not comfortable on that brawler. Personal mode helps here, and the selection-effect bias is a known limitation (see STATUS.md).

Interpretation:
- Rainbow lines up with patch upgrades: all 6 new-Buffie reworks, Vince's new Hypercharge, and buffs to Shelly, Draco, Hank, Chuck, Melodie and Squeak.
- Red is on 8 of the 12 nerfed brawlers. Amber, Wendy, Rico and Pierce have no arrow.
- Green or rainbow is on 9 of the 10 buffed brawlers. Buster has no arrow.
- But arrows also mark unchanged brawlers (e.g. El Primo, Colt, Mortis, Kaze, Bolt red; Ruffs, Meeple, Penny, Crow green). So the arrows look like **tier movement since Ash's previous list** (partly patch-driven), not a strict buff/nerf legend.
- `patch.json` keeps the official notes as the source of truth for "changed brawlers". Nothing in the image contradicts the notes, so no change is needed there.
